# Quality Antenatal Care Coverage: Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey 2022

**Note:** This workflow was created to fulfill a coursework assignment. I recommend reading the necessary documentation as the main reference to understand the sampling, data collection process and the potential limitations which remains important in interpreting the findings. 

**Dataset:** The primary dataset used in this project is the **Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey (BDHS) 2022** data. 
*   **Target Population:** Women aged 15–49 who had a live birth in the 3 years preceding the survey.
*   **Dependent Variable (Quality ANC):** Quality ANC was a binary variable, defined as attending at least four ANC visits, with at least one performed by a skilled medical provider, including weight and blood pressure measurements, blood and urine tests, and during pregnancy, counseling on potential danger signs minimum once.
*   Variables: Six demographic and socioeconomic factors were considered as independent variables,
and selection was made based on previous studies (8,11,12). The analysis used categories already
available in the BDHS recode data file except for age. There were eight divisions (Barishal,
Chattogram, Dhaka, Khulna, Mymensingh, Rajshahi, Rangpur, and Sylhet), five wealth quintiles
(richest, richer, middle, poorer, and poorest), five education levels (primary incomplete, primary
complete, secondary incomplete, secondary and higher completed, and no education), residence
(urban and rural) and mobile ownership (yes and no). Three maternal age groups (<20 years, 20–
34, and 35–49 years) were generated following previous maternal health studies (14,15).
* Due to the usage agreements with the DHS Program, the raw dataset is not available in this repository which can be requested from the DHS Program website (https://dhsprogram.com/methodology/survey/survey-display-584.cfm).
